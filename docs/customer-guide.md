# Manufacturing Automation Platform - Customer Guide

## 1. Getting Started
Welcome to your Enterprise Operations platform. Upon receiving your invite, complete your **Organization Setup** to define your base timezone, currency, and primary contact.

## 2. Workflows & Automation
The platform includes built-in automation templates:
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Automatically issue a Purchase Requirement when inventory drops below threshold.
- **Invoice Processing**: Route inbound emails through Document AI for automated ledger reconciliation.

## 3. Subscription & Billing
You can manage your limits under Settings -> Subscription. Usage meters (such as AI Tokens and API endpoints) reset dynamically per billing cycle.
